## Next focus

The root of sub-agent collaboration is **task understanding → decomposition →
acceptance**:

1. A structured task contract: deliverables, constraints, non-goals,
   acceptance, sources.
2. Decomposition into Work Items with objective, inputs, outputs,
   dependencies, acceptance criteria and artifacts.
3. Independent acceptance: verifier ≠ executor, recomputed artifact evidence,
   acceptance receipt bound to the Work Item and plan.

Start with MA-003 (acceptance hardening) and wire the Work Item acceptance
criteria/artifact checks into the existing `acceptance_gate`/`verify_artifact`
primitives. Then MA-005/006 so a stalled or fake worker cannot silently pass.
