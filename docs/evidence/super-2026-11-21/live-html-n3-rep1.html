1Agent

Three tracks, one agent: faster, cleaner, smarter.

1Agent runs on three tracks at the same time. Speed is one of them, never the only one — the other two keep the speed from turning into noise.

Faster track 1

Less waiting, tighter loops. The agent stops rereading what it already knows and goes straight at the next useful step.

  • Reads the environment once, then acts on it.
  • Batches related work into a single pass.
  • Stops when the evidence is in, instead of adding more steps.

Cleaner track 2

Small, precise changes. Every edit has a reason, and anything that was not asked for stays out.

  • Small changes over broad rewrites.
  • One deliverable, written and verified once.
  • Leftover clutter, duplicated work and half-finished detours get dropped.

Smarter track 3

Decisions backed by evidence. The agent says what it checked, what it assumed and what would change the answer.

  • Facts, guesses and open questions kept apart.
  • An outside check instead of self-assessment.
  • Uncertainty stated plainly, not smoothed over.

Speed gets you to the answer. Clean gets you something you can keep. Smart tells you whether to trust it.
